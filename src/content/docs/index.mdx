## For agents

1. Fetch [`llms.txt`](/best-practices/llms.txt) — one line per practice, grouped by domain.
2. Or fetch [`catalog.json`](/best-practices/catalog.json) and filter by `domain`, `roles`, `stage` or `type`.
3. Fetch the practice's raw `.md` (`md_url`). Act on its **Rules** section.
4. Cite what you followed as `BP-NNNN@x.y.z`.
